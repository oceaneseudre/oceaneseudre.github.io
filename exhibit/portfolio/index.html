Morphtrace

I developed an app to explore the gene regulatory network during development in Bicyclus anynana, a tropical butterfly that exhibits different morphs in the wet and dry seasons. It includes features for exploring differential expression across developmental stages and morphs, and allows users to generate QC plots, heatmaps, volcano plots, co-expression networks, gene-level expression time courses, as well as gene ontology and pathway enrichment analyses. The app will soon be openly available to everyone.
