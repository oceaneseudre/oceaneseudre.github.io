Summary

  • Bioinformatics and evolutionary genomics researcher with expertise in large-scale genomic data analysis and transcriptomics. Experienced in integrating multi-omics datasets to study trait evolution & ecological adaptation. Alongside in silico work, I have led field research and delivered training and outreach in LMIC contexts. In the past, you could find me in the wet lab for all things developmental-processes related. Comitted to ethical, transparent, and equitable research practices.

Current position

  • 2022 - Present: Postdoctoral Researcher Associate — Oostra Lab, QMUL (UK)

  • Lead researcher, focusing on the evolution of phenotypic plasticity in tropical butterflies. Developed bioinformatic pipelines for standardised, reproducible genomes de novo assembly & annotation. Conducted large scale comparative genomics & transcriptomics and signature of selection to understand ecological trait evolution. Led multiple fieldworks expedition to the Cameroonian rainforest and developed outreach inititaves & bioinformatic workshops with local collaborators. I was awarded the L’Oréal–UNESCO For Women in Science Prize for this work.

Education

  • 2018 - 2022: PhD in evo-devo — Supervisor: Martin-Chema Duran, QMUL (UK)

  • Research on the evolution of developmental mode using molecular wet and dry lab techniques. Investigated cell fate specification and body patterning in Annelida - showing that ERK1/2 signalling specify the embryonic organiser cell. Mapped downstream gene regulatory networks both temporally (RNA-seq time-course analysis) and spatially (in situ hybridisation). Contributed to a larger functional genomics project on the origin of bilaterian life cycles by resolving the Hox genes repertoire in silico.

  • 2016 - 2018: MSc in Ecology, Biodiversity & Evolution — Natural History Museum of Paris (France). Graduated first in cohort.

Technical

  • Dry lab:

    • Genomics & transcriptomics: Design reproducible pipelines for de novo genome assembly and annotation across many species (long & short reads sequencing) and comparative analyses. Full RNA-seq/ATAC- seq workflows and integration of expression data with gene network analyses, gene ontology & pathway enrichment.

    • Selection & adaptation: Analyse adaptive processes within genomes & across species and populations. Run SNP, SV, and CNV calling, population structure analyses and selection scans.

    • Reproducibility & computing: Proficient in R and Bash, familiar with Python. Experienced with GitHub/GitLab, RMarkdown, HPC/UNIX and workflow automation.

  • Wet lab:

    • Extensive experience in experimental evolution, experimental design, phenotypic characterisation, in situ hybridisation, immunostaining, PCR, RNA/DNA extraction, probe design, molecular cloning, RNA-seq/ ATAC-seq lab workflows and microscopy.

  • Data visualisation & science translation:

    • Strong in data visualisation, digital design and illustration. Led multiple biodiversity outreach events. Developed and distributed illustrated field guides for local schools across fieldwork sites.
    • Mentored BCs and MCs students.
    • Delivered bioinformatics training and hands-on data-analysis courses in LMIC settings.

  • Field work:

    • Led multiple 3-week+ expeditions in remote tropical ecosystems. Managed sampling design, identification, logistics, ethics, permits and team.
