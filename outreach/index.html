Birds and butterflies of Cameroon, 2025

About

I designed outreach material for primary schools across Cameroon & delivered activities to both pupils and teachers. This work was conducted in collaboration with Mercy Murkwe, Eric Focam and Esco Ewome from the Univerisity of Buea, where they participated their knowledge and I provided design, printing, organisation & distribution.

We focused on activites about birds & butterflies of Cameroon, the principle of the evolution of species & life cycle in animals and we covered 10 schools in total, reaching to children aged 6 to 12. The pedagogical material was provided in French & English, the two official languages in Cameroon.

Fieldguides for childrens

We made and printed 200 copies of those mini fieldguides. They were distributed to childrens so they can explore and learn to identify the birds and butterflies from their gardens, towns & forests.

Festival of Communities, London 2023

About

I lead an activity at the Festival of Communities in Tower Hamlets, London. The event is organised by QMUL and aims at celebrating the relationship between the academics and the local comunities. It is open to all but designed to be accessible and engaging for families (living + learning together!).

I focused on tropical butterflies, telling visitors about the work done in Vicencio's Oostra lab. We did coloring, science translation, butterfly wings tiles matching, microscope observations..
